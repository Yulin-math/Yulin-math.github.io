About

I am a Senior Research Associate at the School of Mathematics, University of Bristol, working with Dr. Laura Monk and Prof. Jens Marklof. I completed my Ph.D. at Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Long Jin.

Research Interests

  • Spectral Geometry
  • Dynamical Systems
  • Random Surfaces and Graphs
